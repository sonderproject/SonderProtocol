01The Current Moment

The world is becoming more fragile.
Preparedness is no longer extreme — it is responsible.

Modern life runs on tightly coupled systems: power, water, logistics, networks. They are efficient and they are brittle. When one fails, the others follow. These are the risk vectors we track.

FEATURED BRIEFING UPDATED · Q4 2026

Grid demand is outpacing grid capacity.

Aging infrastructure, extreme weather, and surging electricity demand are increasing the odds of extended regional outages. A 72-hour blackout is no longer a rare event — it is a planning baseline.
